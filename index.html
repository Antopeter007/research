Before the next run
Placement & congestion research

Which placement parameter is changing? What is the baseline? Are design, constraints and measurement stage consistent? Which congestion and timing reports will you retain?

Clock tree & timing research

What clock and analysis assumptions are in use? What are the reported stage and corner? Are setup and hold observations recorded separately? How will you distinguish the clock effect from other changed variables?

Power, performance & area evaluation

Are area and power units explicit? What are the power-analysis assumptions? Are the library, voltage, input activity, extraction context and tool settings documented? Which trade-off matters for this experiment?

Reproducibility & publication readiness

Can another researcher reconstruct the run from the recorded inputs, versions and settings? Are limitations and contradictory results included? Are claims supported by the measured evidence?

How saving works

  • Your work is automatically saved in this browser on this device. Returning to this same website brings it back.
  • The saved workspace is separate from the public GitHub repository. Adding a paper does not commit it to GitHub or share it with anyone.
  • Another browser, another device, or private browsing has separate storage. Export here, then restore there to transfer your work.
  • Clearing browser data, browser storage eviction, or leaving a private session can remove saved work. A downloaded backup is the recovery copy.
  • This site does not provide a login or cloud database. Automatic cross-device sync is not currently available.
